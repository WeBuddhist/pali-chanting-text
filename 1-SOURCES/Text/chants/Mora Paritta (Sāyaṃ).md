# Mora Paritta (Sāyaṃ) ^0

Apetayaṃ cakkhumā ekarājā, ^0-1

Harissavaṇṇo paṭhavippabhāso. ^0-2

Taṃ taṃ namassāmi harissavaṇṇaṃ paṭhavippabhāsaṃ, ^0-3

Tayajja guttā viharemu rattiṃ. ^0-4

Ye brāhmaṇā vedagū sabbadhamme, ^0-5

Te me namo te ca maṃ pālayantu. ^0-6

Namatthu buddhānaṃ, namatthu bodhiyā. ^0-7

Namo vimuttānaṃ, namo vimuttiyā. ^0-8

Imaṃ so parittaṃ katvā, ^0-9

Moro vāsamakappayi. ^0-10
