# Parābhava Sutta ^0

Parābhavantaṃ purisaṃ, mayaṃ pucchāma Gotamaṃ.
Bhagavantaṃ pucchāmāsaṃ, kiṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ? ^0-1

Suvijāno bhavaṃ hoti, durvijāno parābhavo.
Dhammakāmo bhavaṃ hoti, dhammadesiṃ parābhavo. ^0-2

Iti hetaṃ vijānāma, paṭhamo so parābhavo.
Dutiyaṃ Bhagavā brūhi, kiṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ? ^0-3

Asantassa piyā honti, sante na kurute piyaṃ.
Asataṃ dhammaṃ rocati, taṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ. ^0-4

Iti hetaṃ vijānāma, dutiyo so parābhavo.
Tatiyaṃ Bhagavā brūhi, kiṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ? ^0-5

Niddāsīlī sabhāsīlī, anuhaṭṭhā ca yo naro.
Alaso kodhapaññāṇo, taṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ. ^0-6

Iti hetaṃ vijānāma, tatiyo so parābhavo.
Catutthaṃ Bhagavā brūhi, kiṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ? ^0-7

Yo mātaraṃ pitaraṃ vā, jiṇṇakaṃ gatayobbanaṃ.
Pahasanto na bharati, te parābhavato mukhaṃ. ^0-8

Iti hetaṃ vijānāma, catuttho so parābhavo.
Pañcamaṃ Bhagavā brūhi, kiṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ? ^0-9

Yo brāhmaṇaṃ vā samaṇaṃ vā, ajjaṃ vā pi vaṇṇitaṃ.
Musāvādena vañceti, taṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ. ^0-10

Iti hetaṃ vijānāma, pañcamo so parābhavo.
Chaṭṭhaṃ Bhagavā brūhi, kiṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ? ^0-11

Pahūtavitto puriso, sahirajjo samojano.
Eko bhuñjati sādūni, taṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ. ^0-12

Iti hetaṃ vijānāma, chaṭṭhamo so parābhavo.
Sattamaṃ Bhagavā brūhi, kiṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ? ^0-13

Jātitthaddho dhanatthaddho, gotatthaddho ca yo naro.
Saññātiṃ atimaññeti, taṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ. ^0-14

Iti hetaṃ vijānāma, sattamo so parābhavo.
Aṭṭhamaṃ Bhagavā brūhi, kiṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ? ^0-15

Itidhutto surādhutto, akkhadhutto ca yo naro.
Laddhaṃ laddhaṃ vināseti, taṃ parābhavato mukhaṃ. ^0-16
