# Tisaraṇa Aṭṭhasīla ^0

Arahaṃ Sammā Sambuddho Bhagavā Buddhaṃ Bhagavantaṃ abhivādemi. ^0-1

Svākkhāto Bhagavatā Dhammo, Dhammaṃ namassāmi. ^0-2

Supaṭipanno Bhagavato Sāvaka Saṅgho, Saṅghaṃ namāmi. ^0-3

Namo Tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa. ^0-4

Namo Tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa. ^0-5

Namo Tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa. ^0-6

Buddhaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-7

Dhammaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-8

Saṅghaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-9

Dutiyampi Buddhaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-10

Dutiyampi Dhammaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-11

Dutiyampi Saṅghaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-12

Tatiyampi Buddhaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-13

Tatiyampi Dhammaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-14

Tatiyampi Saṅghaṃ Saraṇaṃ Gacchāmi. ^0-15

Bhante: Tisaraṇagamanaṃ Sampuṇṇaṃ. ^0-16

**Aṭṭha Sīlāni** ^0-17

Pāṇātipātā Veramaṇī Sikkhāpadaṃ Samādiyāmi. ^0-18

Adinnādānā Veramaṇī Sikkhāpadaṃ Samādiyāmi. ^0-19

Abrahmacariyā Veramaṇī Sikkhāpadaṃ Samādiyāmi. ^0-20

Musāvādā Veramaṇī Sikkhāpadaṃ Samādiyāmi. ^0-21

Surā-Meraya-Majja-Pamādaṭṭhānā Veramaṇī Sikkhāpadaṃ Samādiyāmi. ^0-22

Vikāla-Bhojanā Veramaṇī Sikkhāpadaṃ Samādiyāmi. ^0-23

Nacca-Gīta-Vādita-Visūka-Dassanā, Mālā-Gandha-Vilepana-Dhāraṇa-Maṇḍana-Vibhūsanaṭṭhānā Veramaṇī Sikkhāpadaṃ Samādiyāmi. ^0-24

Uccāsayanā-Mahāsayanā Veramaṇī Sikkhāpadaṃ Samādiyāmi. ^0-25

Bhante: Tisaraṇena Saha Aṭṭhaṅga-Samannāgataṃ Uposatha Sīlaṃ Dhammaṃ Sādhukaṃ Surakkhitaṃ Katvā Appamādena Sampādetha. ^0-26

Upāsaka: Āma Bhante. Sādhu, Sādhu, Sādhu. ^0-27
