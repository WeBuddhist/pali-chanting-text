# Jinapañjara Paritta ^0

Jayāsana gatā vīrā, jetvā Māraṃ savāhiniṃ. ^0-1

Catusaccāmatarasaṃ, ye piviṃsu narāsabhā. ॥1॥ ^0-2

Taṇhaṅkarādayo Buddhā, aṭṭhavīsati nāyakā. ^0-3

Sabbe patiṭṭhitā mayhaṃ, matthake me munissarā. ॥2॥ ^0-4

Sire patiṭṭhitā Buddhā, dhammo ca tava locane. ^0-5

Saṅgho patiṭṭhito mayhaṃ, ure sabbaguṇākaro. ॥3॥ ^0-6

Hadaye Anuruddho ca, Sāriputto ca dakkhiṇe. ^0-7

Koṇḍañño piṭṭhibhāgasmiṃ, Moggallāno'si vāmake. ॥4॥ ^0-8

Dakkhiṇe savaṇe mayhaṃ, ahuṃ Ānandarāhulā. ^0-9

Kassapo ca Mahānāmo, ubhosuṃ vāmasotake. ॥5॥ ^0-10

Kesante piṭṭhibhāgasmiṃ, suriyo viya pabhaṅkaro. ^0-11

Nisinno sirisampanno, Sobhito munipuṅgavo. ॥6॥ ^0-12

Kumārakassapo nāma, mahesī citravādako. ^0-13

So mayhaṃ vadane niccaṃ, patiṭṭhāsi guṇākaro. ॥7॥ ^0-14

Puṇṇo Aṅgulimālo ca, Upālinandasīvalī. ^0-15

Therā pañca ime jātā, lalāṭe tilakā mama. ॥8॥ ^0-16

Sesāsīti mahātherā, vijitā jinasāvakā. ^0-17

Jalantā sīlatejena, aṅgamaṅgesu saṇṭhitā. ॥9॥ ^0-18

Ratanaṃ purato āsi, dakkhiṇe Mettasuttakaṃ. ^0-19

Dhajaggaṃ pacchato āsi, vāme Aṅgulimālakaṃ. ॥10॥ ^0-20

Khandhamoraparittañca, Āṭānāṭiya suttakaṃ. ^0-21

Ākāsacchadanaṃ āsi, sesā pākārasaññitā. ॥11॥ ^0-22

Jināṇābalasaṃyute, dhammapākāralaṅkate. ^0-23

Vasato me catukiccena, sadā Sambuddhapañjare. ॥12॥ ^0-24

Vātapittādi sañjātā, bāhirajjhattupaddavā. ^0-25

Asesā vilayaṃ yantu, anantaguṇatejasā. ॥13॥ ^0-26

Jinapañjaramajjhaṭṭhaṃ, viharantaṃ mahītale. ^0-27

Sadā pālentu maṃ sabbe, te mahāpurisāsabhā. ॥14॥ ^0-28

Iccevamaccantakato surakkho, jinānubhāvena jitūpapaddavo. ^0-29

Buddhānubhāvena hatārisagho, carāsi saddhammanubhāvapālito. ॥15॥ ^0-30

Iccevamaccantakato surakkho, jinānubhāvena jitūpapaddavo. ^0-31

Dhammānubhāvena hatārisagho, carāsi saddhammanubhāvapālito. ॥16॥ ^0-32

Iccevamaccantakato surakkho, jinānubhāvena jitūpapaddavo. ^0-33

Saṅghānubhāvena hatārisagho, carāsi saddhammanubhāvapālito. ॥17॥ ^0-34

Saddhammapākāra-parikkhitosi, aṭṭhariyā aṭṭhadisāsu honti. ^0-35

Etthantare aṭṭhanāyā bhavanti, uddhaṃ vitānaṃ va jinā ṭhitā me. ॥18॥ ^0-36

Bhindato Mārasenaṃ tava sirasi ṭhito, Bodhimāruhya Satthā. ^0-37

Moggallāno'si vāme vasati bhujaṅgaṭe, dakkhiṇe Sāriputto. ^0-38

Dhammo majjhe urasmiṃ viharati bhavato, mokkhato morayoniṃ. ^0-39

Sampatto Bodhisatto caraṇayugagato, bhānu lokekanātho. ॥19॥ ^0-40

Sabbāvamaṅgalamupaddavadunimittaṃ, sabbītīrogagahadosasamasesanindā. ^0-41

Sabbantarāyabhayadussupinaṃ akantaṃ, Buddhānubhāvapavarena payātu nāsaṃ. ॥20॥ ^0-42

Sabbāvamaṅgalamupaddavadunimittaṃ, sabbītīrogagahadosasamasesanindā. ^0-43

Sabbantarāyabhayadussupinaṃ akantaṃ, Dhammānubhāvapavarena payātu nāsaṃ. ॥21॥ ^0-44

Sabbāvamaṅgalamupaddavadunimittaṃ, sabbītīrogagahadosasamasesanindā. ^0-45

Sabbantarāyabhayadussupinaṃ akantaṃ, Saṅghānubhāvapavarena payātu nāsaṃ. ॥22॥ ^0-46
