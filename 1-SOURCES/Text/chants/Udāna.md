# Udāna ^0

Anekajātisaṃsāraṃ, sandhāvissaṃ anibbisaṃ. ^0-1

Gahakārakaṃ gavesanto, dukkhā jāti punappunaṃ. ^0-2

Gahakāraka diṭṭhosi, puna gehaṃ na kāhasi. ^0-3

Sabbā te phāsukā bhaggā, gahakūṭaṃ visaṅkhitaṃ. ^0-4

Visaṅkhāragataṃ cittaṃ, taṇhānaṃ khayam ajjhagā. ^0-5
