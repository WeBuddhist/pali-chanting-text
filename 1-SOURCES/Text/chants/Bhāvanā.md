# Bhāvanā ^0

Devo vassatu kālena, sassasampatti hetu ca. ^0-1

Pīto bhavatu loko ca, rājā bhavatu dhammiko. ^0-2

Imāya buddhapūjāya katāya suddhacetasā. ^0-3

Ciraṃ tiṭṭhatu saddhammo, loko hotu sukhī sadā. ^0-4

Imāya dhammapūjāya katāya suddhacetasā. ^0-5

Ciraṃ tiṭṭhatu saddhammo, loko hotu sukhī sadā. ^0-6

Imāya saṅghapūjāya katāya suddhacetasā. ^0-7

Ciraṃ tiṭṭhatu saddhammo, loko hotu sukhī sadā. ^0-8
