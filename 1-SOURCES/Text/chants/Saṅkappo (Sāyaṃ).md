# Saṅkappo (Sāyaṃ) ^0

Imāya dhammānudhamma-paṭipattiyā Buddhaṃ pūjemi. ^0-1

Imāya dhammānudhamma-paṭipattiyā Dhammaṃ pūjemi. ^0-2

Imāya dhammānudhamma-paṭipattiyā Saṅghaṃ pūjemi. ^0-3

Addhā imāya paṭipattiyā jāti-jarā-maraṇamhā parimuccissāmi. ^0-4

Iminā puññakammena mā me bāla-samāgamo. ^0-5

Sataṃ samāgamo hotu yāva Nibbāna-pattiyā. ^0-6
