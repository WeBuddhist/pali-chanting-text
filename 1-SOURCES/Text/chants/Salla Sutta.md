# Salla Sutta ^0

Animittam anaññātaṃ, maccānaṃ idha jīvitaṃ.
Kasirañca parittañca, tañca dukkhena saṃyutaṃ. ^0-1

Na hi so upakkamo atthi, yena jātā na miyyare.
Jarampi patvā maraṇaṃ, evaṃdhammā hi pāṇino. ^0-2

Phalānamiva pakkānaṃ, pāto papatanato bhayaṃ.
Evaṃ jātānaṃ maccānaṃ, niccaṃ maraṇato bhayaṃ. ^0-3

Yathāpi kumbhakārassa, katā mattikabhājanā.
Sabbe bhedanapariyantā, evaṃ maccāna jīvitaṃ. ^0-4

Daharā ca mahantā ca, ye bālā ye ca paṇḍitā.
Sabbe maccuvasaṃ yanti, sabbe maccuparāyanā. ^0-5

Tesaṃ maccuparetānaṃ, gacchataṃ paralokato.
Na pitā tāyate puttaṃ, ñātī vā pana ñātake. ^0-6

Pekkhataṃ yeva ñātīnaṃ, passa lālapataṃ puthu.
Ekameko va maccānaṃ, go vajjho viya niyyati. ^0-7

Evamabbhāhato loko, maccunā ca jarāya ca.
Tasmā dhīrā na socanti, viditvā lokapariyāyaṃ. ^0-8

Yassa maggaṃ na jānāsi, āgatassa gatassa vā.
Ubho ante asampassaṃ, niratthaṃ paridevasi. ^0-9

Paridevayamāno ce, kañcidatthaṃ udabbahe.
Sammūḷho hiṃsamattānaṃ, kayirā c'enaṃ vicakkhaṇo. ^0-10

Na hi ruṇṇena sokena, santiṃ pappoti cetaso.
Bhiyyassuppajjate dukkhaṃ, sarīrañcūpahaññati. ^0-11

Kiso vivaṇṇo bhavati, hiṃsamattānamattanā.
Na tena petā pālenti, niratthā paridevanā. ^0-12

Sokamappajahaṃ jantu, bhiyyo dukkhaṃ nigacchati.
Anutthunanto kālakataṃ, sokassa vasamanvagū. ^0-13

Aññepi passa gāmine, yathākammupage nare.
Maccuno vasamāgamma, phandante c'idha pāṇino. ^0-14

Yena yena hi maññanti, tato taṃ hoti aññathā.
Etādiso vinābhāvo, passa lokassa pariyāyaṃ. ^0-15

Api ce vassasataṃ jīve, bhiyyo vā pana mānavo.
Ñātisaṅghā vinā hoti, jahāti idha jīvitaṃ. ^0-16

Tasmā arahato sutvā, vineyya paridevitaṃ.
Petaṃ kālakataṃ disvā, na so labbhā mayā iti. ^0-17

Yathā saraṇamādittaṃ, vārinā parinibbaye.
Evampi dhīro sappañño, paṇḍito kusalo naro. ^0-18

Khippamuppatitaṃ sokaṃ, vāto tūlaṃ va dhaṃsaye. ^0-19

Paridevaṃ pajappañca, domanassañca attano.
Attano sukhamesāno, abbahe sallamattano. ^0-20

Abbūḷhasallo asito, santiṃ pappuyya cetaso.
Sabbasokaṃ atikkanto, asoko hoti nibbutoti. ^0-21
