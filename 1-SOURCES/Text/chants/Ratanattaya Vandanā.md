# Ratanattaya Vandanā ^0

Namāmi Buddhaṃ Guṇasāgaraṃ Taṃ ^0-1

Satthā Sadā Hontu Sukhī Averā ^0-2

Kāyo Jiguccho Sakalo Duggandho ^0-3

Gacchanti Sabbe Maraṇaṃ Ahañca ^0-4

Namāmi Dhammaṃ Sugatena Desitaṃ ^0-5

Satthā Sadā Hontu Sukhī Averā ^0-6

Kāyo Jiguccho Sakalo Duggandho ^0-7

Gacchanti Sabbe Maraṇaṃ Ahañca ^0-8

Namāmi Saṅghaṃ Munirāja Sāvakaṃ ^0-9

Satthā Sadā Hontu Sukhī Averā ^0-10

Kāyo Jiguccho Sakalo Duggandho ^0-11

Gacchanti Sabbe Maraṇaṃ Ahañca ^0-12

Namāmi Paccekabuddhaṃ Visuddhaṃ ^0-13

Satthā Sadā Hontu Sukhī Averā ^0-14

Kāyo Jiguccho Sakalo Duggandho ^0-15

Gacchanti Sabbe Maraṇaṃ Ahañca ^0-16
