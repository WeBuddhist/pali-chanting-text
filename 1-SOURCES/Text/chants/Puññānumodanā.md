# Puññānumodanā ^0

Dukkhappattā ca niddukkhā, bhayappattā ca nibbhayā. ^0-1

Sokappattā ca nissokā, hontu sabbepi pāṇino. ॥1॥ ^0-2

Etāvatā ca amhehi, sambhataṃ puññasampadaṃ. ^0-3

Sabbe devā anumodantu, sabbasampatti siddhiyā. ॥2॥ ^0-4

Dānaṃ dadantu saddhāya, sīlaṃ rakkhantu sabbadā. ^0-5

Bhāvanābhiratā hontu, gacchantu devatāgatā. ॥3॥ ^0-6

Sabbe buddhā balappattā, paccekānañca yaṃ balaṃ. ^0-7

Arahantānañca tejena, rakkhaṃ bandhāmi sabbaso. ॥4॥ ^0-8

Sabbe dhammā balappattā, paccekānañca yaṃ balaṃ. ^0-9

Arahantānañca tejena, rakkhaṃ bandhāmi sabbaso. ॥5॥ ^0-10

Sabbe saṅghā balappattā, paccekānañca yaṃ balaṃ. ^0-11

Arahantānañca tejena, rakkhaṃ bandhāmi sabbaso. ॥6॥ ^0-12

Ākāsaṭṭhā ca bhummaṭṭhā, devā nāgā mahiddhikā. ^0-13

Puññaṃ taṃ anumoditvā, ciraṃ rakkhantu buddhasāsanaṃ. ॥7॥ ^0-14

Ākāsaṭṭhā ca bhummaṭṭhā, devā nāgā mahiddhikā. ^0-15

Puññaṃ taṃ anumoditvā, ciraṃ rakkhantu dhammadesanaṃ. ॥8॥ ^0-16

Ākāsaṭṭhā ca bhummaṭṭhā, devā nāgā mahiddhikā. ^0-17

Puññaṃ taṃ anumoditvā, ciraṃ rakkhantu maṃ parittaṃ. ॥9॥ ^0-18
