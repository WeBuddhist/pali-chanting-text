# Dhammapālana Gāthā ^0

Sabba pāpassa akaraṇaṃ. ^0-1

Kusalassa upasampadā. ^0-2

Sacittapariyodapanaṃ, etaṃ buddhāna sāsanaṃ. ^0-3

Dhammaṃ care sucaritaṃ, na taṃ duccaritaṃ care. ^0-4

Dhammacārī sukhaṃ seti, asmiṃ loke paramhi ca. ^0-5

Na tāvatā dhammadharo, yāvatā bahu bhāsati. ^0-6

Yo ca appampi sutvāna, dhammaṃ kāyena passati. ^0-7

Sa ve dhammadharo hoti, yo dhammaṃ nappamajjati. ^0-8

Natthi me saraṇaṃ añña, Buddho me saraṇaṃ varaṃ. ^0-9

Etena saccavajjena, hotu me jayamaṅgalaṃ. ^0-10

Natthi me saraṇaṃ añña, Dhammo me saraṇaṃ varaṃ. ^0-11

Etena saccavajjena, hotu me jayamaṅgalaṃ. ^0-12

Natthi me saraṇaṃ añña, Saṅgho me saraṇaṃ varaṃ. ^0-13

Etena saccavajjena, hotu me jayamaṅgalaṃ. ^0-14

Namo Buddhāya. ^0-15

Namo Dhammāya. ^0-16

Namo Saṅghāya. ^0-17

Sādhu. Sādhu. Sādhu. ^0-18
