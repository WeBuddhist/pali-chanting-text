# Saṅkappo ^0

Imāya dhammānudhamma-paṭipattiyā buddhaṃ pūjemi. ^0-1

Imāya dhammānudhamma-paṭipattiyā dhammaṃ pūjemi. ^0-2

Imāya dhammānudhamma-paṭipattiyā saṅghaṃ pūjemi. ^0-3

Addhā imāya paṭipattiyā jātijarāmaraṇamhā parimuccissāmi. ^0-4

Iminā puññakammena mā me bālasamāgamo. ^0-5

Sataṃ samāgamo hotu, yāva nibbānapattiyā. ^0-6
