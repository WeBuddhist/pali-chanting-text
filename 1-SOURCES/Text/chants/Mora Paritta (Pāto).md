# Mora Paritta (Pāto) ^0

Udetayaṃ cakkhumā ekarājā, harissavaṇṇo pathavippabhāso. ^0-1

Taṃ taṃ namassāmi harissavaṇṇaṃ pathavippabhāsaṃ, ^0-2

Tayajja guttā viharemu divasaṃ. ॥1॥ ^0-3

Ye brāhmaṇā vedagū sabbadhamme, ^0-4

Te me namo te ca maṃ pālayantu. ^0-5

Namatthu buddhānaṃ namatthu bodhiyā, ^0-6

Namo vimuttānaṃ namo vimuttiyā. ^0-7

Imaṃ so parittaṃ katvā, moro carati esanā. ॥2॥ ^0-8
