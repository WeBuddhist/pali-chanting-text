# Nava-guṇa Gāthā ^0

Arahaṃ arahoti nāmena, arahaṃ pāpaṃ na kāraye. ^0-1

Arahattaphalaṃ patto, arahaṃ nāma te namo. ^0-2

Sammā-sambuddha ñāṇena, sammā-sambuddha desanā. ^0-3

Sammā-sambuddha lokasmiṃ, sammā-sambuddha te namo. ^0-4

Vijjā-caraṇa sampanno, tassa vijjā pakāsitā. ^0-5

Atītā-nāgā tuppanno, vijjā-caraṇa te namo. ^0-6

Sugato sugatatānaṃ, sugato sundarampi ca. ^0-7

Nibbānaṃ sugati yanti, sugato nāma te namo. ^0-8

Lokavidūti nāmena, atītā-nāgate vidū. ^0-9

Saṅkhāra sata mekāse, lokavidū nāma te namo. ^0-10

Anuttaro ñāṇasīlena, yo lokassa anuttaro. ^0-11

Anuttaro pūja lokasmiṃ, taṃ namassāmi anuttaro. ^0-12

Sārathī-sārathī devā, yo lokassa susārathī. ^0-13

Sārathī pūja lokasmiṃ, taṃ namassāmi sārathī. ^0-14

Deva-cakkava manussānaṃ, loke agga phala dadaṃ. ^0-15

Dadantaṃ damayantānaṃ, purisā jeṭṭha te namo. ^0-16

Bhagavā bhagavā yutto, bhaggaṃ kilesa vāhato. ^0-17

Bhaggaṃ saṃsāra muttāro, bhagavā nāma te namo. ^0-18

Sādhu! Sādhu! Sādhu! ^0-19
