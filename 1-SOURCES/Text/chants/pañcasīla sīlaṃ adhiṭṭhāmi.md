# pañcasīla sīlaṃ adhiṭṭhāmi ^0


## Namo Tassa ^1-0

Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa.
Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa.
Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammāsambuddhassa. ^1-1

## Tisaraṇa ^2-0

Buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.
Dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.
Saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi. ^2-1

Dutiyampi buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.
Dutiyampi dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.
Dutiyampi saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi. ^2-2

Tatiyampi buddhaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.
Tatiyampi dhammaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi.
Tatiyampi saṅghaṃ saraṇaṃ gacchāmi. ^2-3

## Pañcasīlāni ^3-0

Pāṇātipātā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ samādiyāmi. ^3-1

Adinnādānā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ samādiyāmi. ^3-2

Kāmesu micchācārā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ samādiyāmi. ^3-3

Musāvādā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ samādiyāmi. ^3-4

Surāmeraya-majja-pamādaṭṭhānā veramaṇī sikkhāpadaṃ samādiyāmi. ^3-5
 
## Ovāda ^4-0

Tisaraṇena saha pañcasīlaṃ dhammaṃ sādhukaṃ surakkhitaṃ katvā appamādena sampādetha. ^4-1

Āma bhante. ^4-2

Sādhu! Sādhu! Sādhu! ^4-3
