# Bodhi Pūjā ^0

Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammā sambuddhassa. ^0-1

Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammā sambuddhassa. ^0-2

Namo tassa bhagavato arahato sammā sambuddhassa. ^0-3

Vandāmi cetiyaṃ sabbaṃ, sabbaṭhānesu patiṭṭhitaṃ. ^0-4

Sārīrika dhātu Mahābodhi, Buddharūpaṃ sakalaṃ sadā. ^0-5

Yas mūle nisinnovā, sabbāri vijayaṃ akā. ^0-6

Patto sabbaññutaṃ Satthā, vande taṃ Bodhi pādapaṃ. ^0-7

Ime ete Mahābodhi, Lokanāthena pūjitā. ^0-8

Ahaṃ pi te namassāmi, Bodhirājā namaṭṭhu te. ^0-9

Bodhirājā namaṭṭhu te, Bodhirājā namaṭṭhu te. ^0-10

Paṭhamaṃ Bodhi pallaṅkaṃ, dutiyañca animisaṃ. ^0-11

Tatiyaṃ caṅkamaṇaṃ seṭṭhaṃ, catutthaṃ ratanāgharaṃ. ^0-12

Pañcamaṃ Ajapālañca, Mucalindena chaṭṭhamaṃ. ^0-13

Sattamaṃ Rājāyatanaṃ, vande taṃ muni sevitaṃ. ^0-14

Mahādayo Mahāsatto, bodhimūle nisīdiya. ^0-15

Pañcamāre parājetvā, patto sambodhimuttamaṃ. ^0-16

Etena saccavajjena, sotthi me hotu sabbadā. ^0-17

Sabbe saṅkhārā aniccāti, yadā paññāya passati. ^0-18

Atha nibbindati dukkhe, esa maggo visuddhiyā. ^0-19

Sabbe saṅkhārā dukkhāti, yadā paññāya passati. ^0-20

Atha nibbindati dukkhe, esa maggo visuddhiyā. ^0-21

Sabbe dhammā anattāti, yadā paññāya passati. ^0-22

Atha nibbindati dukkhe, esa maggo visuddhiyā. ^0-23

Mahākāruṇiko Nātho, hitāya sabbapāṇinaṃ. ^0-24

Puretvā pāramī sabbā, patto sambodhimuttamaṃ. ^0-25

Etena saccavajjena, hotu me jayamaṅgalaṃ. ^0-26

Jayanto bodhiyā mūle, Sākyānaṃ Nandivaḍḍhano. ^0-27

Evaṃ mayaṃ jayo hotu, jayassu jayamaṅgalaṃ. ^0-28

Aparājita pallaṅke, sīse paṭhavi pokkhare. ^0-29

Abhiseke sabba Buddhānaṃ, aggapattō pamodati. ^0-30

Sunakkhattaṃ sumaṅgalaṃ, suppabhātaṃ suhuṭṭhitaṃ. ^0-31

Sukhaṇo sumuhutto ca, suyiṭṭhaṃ brahmacārisu. ^0-32

Padakkhiṇaṃ kāyakammaṃ, vacīkammaṃ padakkhiṇaṃ. ^0-33

Padakkhiṇaṃ manokammaṃ, paṇihite padakkhiṇaṃ. ^0-34

Padakkhiṇāni katvāna, labhante padakkhiṇā. ^0-35

Te ataladdhā sukhitā, viruḷhā Buddhasāsane. ^0-36

Asokā sirikhitā hotha, saha sabbehi jātibhi. ^0-37

Te ataladdhā sukhitā, viruḷhā Buddhasāsane. ^0-38

Abhayā sukhitā hotha, saha sabbehi jātibhi. ^0-39

Te ataladdhā sukhitā, viruḷhā Buddhasāsane. ^0-40
