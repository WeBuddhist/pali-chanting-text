# Khandha Paritta ^0

Virūpakkhehi me mettaṃ, mettaṃ erāpathehi me. ^0-1

Chabyāputtehi me mettaṃ, mettaṃ kaṇhāgotamakehi ca. ॥3॥ ^0-2

Apādakehi me mettaṃ, mettaṃ dipādakehi me. ^0-3

Catuppadehi me mettaṃ, mettaṃ bahuppadehi me. ॥4॥ ^0-4

Mā maṃ apādako hiṃsi, mā maṃ hiṃsi dipādako. ^0-5

Mā maṃ catuppado hiṃsi, mā maṃ hiṃsi bahuppado. ॥5॥ ^0-6

Sabbe sattā sabbe pāṇā, sabbe bhūtā ca kevalā. ^0-7

Sabbe bhadrāṇi passantu, mā kiñci pāpamāgamā. ॥6॥ ^0-8

Appamāṇo buddho, appamāṇo dhammo, appamāṇo saṅgho. ^0-9

Pamāṇavantāni siriṃsapāni, ahivicchikā satapadī uṇṇānābhi sarabhū. ^0-10

Mūsikā, katā me rakkhā, katā me parittā. ^0-11

Paṭikkamantu bhūtāni, sohaṃ namo bhagavato. ^0-12

Namo sattannaṃ sammāsambuddhānanti. ॥ ^0-13
