# Āṭānāṭiya Sutta ^0

Vipassissa ca namatthu, cakkhumantassa sirīmato. ^0-1

Sikhissāpi ca namatthu, sabbabhūtānukampino. ॥1॥ ^0-2

Vessabhussa ca namatthu, ñātakassa tapassino. ^0-3

Namatthu Kakusandhassa, Mārasenappamaddino. ॥2॥ ^0-4

Koṇāgamanassa namatthu, brāhmaṇassa dussimato. ^0-5

Kassapassa namatthu, vippamuttassa sabbadhi. ॥3॥ ^0-6

Aṅgīrasassa namatthu, Sakyaputtassa sirīmato. ^0-7

Yo imaṃ dhammaṃ desesi, sabbadukkhā panūdanaṃ. ॥4॥ ^0-8

Ye cāpi nibbutā loke, yathābhūtaṃ vipassisuṃ. ^0-9

Te janā apisuṇṇātha, mahantaṃ vītasāradā. ॥5॥ ^0-10

Hitaṃ devamanussānaṃ, yaṃ namassanti Gotamaṃ. ^0-11

Vijjācaraṇasampannaṃ, mahantaṃ vītasāradaṃ. ॥6॥ ^0-12

Ete caññe ca sambuddhā, anekasatakoṭiyo. ^0-13

Sabbe Buddhā samasamā, sabbe Buddhā mahiddhikā. ॥7॥ ^0-14

Sabbe dasabalūpetā, tejārajjheṭṭhapāgatā. ^0-15

Sabbe te paṭijānanti, āsabhaṃ ṭhānamuttamaṃ. ॥8॥ ^0-16

Sīhanādaṃ nadantete, parisāsu visāradā. ^0-17

Brahmacakkaṃ pavattenti, loke appaṭivattiyaṃ. ॥9॥ ^0-18

Upetā Buddhadhammehi, aṭṭhārasahi nāyakā. ^0-19

Battiṃsa lakkhaṇūpetā, sītānuvyañjanā dharā. ॥10॥ ^0-20

Vyāmappabhā supabhā, sabbe te munikuñjarā. ^0-21

Buddhā sabbaguṇūpetā, sabbe khīṇāsavā jinā. ॥11॥ ^0-22

Mahāpabhā mahātejā, mahāpaññā mahābalā. ^0-23

Mahākassapikā therā, sabbesānaṃ sukhāvahā. ॥12॥ ^0-24

Dīpā nāthā patiṭṭhā ca, tāṇā leṇā ca pāṇinaṃ. ^0-25

Gati bandhu mahesāsā, saraṇā ca hitesino. ॥13॥ ^0-26

Sadevakasassa lokassa, sabbe ete parāyaṇā. ^0-27

Tesāhaṃ sirasā pāde, vandāmi purisuttame. ॥14॥ ^0-28

Vacasā manasā ceva, vandāme Tathāgataṃ. ^0-29

Sayane āsane ṭhāne, gamane cāpi sabbadā. ॥15॥ ^0-30

Sadā sukhena rakkhantu, Buddhā santikarā mama. ^0-31

Tepi maṃ rakkhito santo, mutto sabbabhayehi ca. ॥16॥ ^0-32

Sabarogā vinimmutto, sabbasantāpavajjito. ^0-33

Sabbaverasamativakanto, nibbuto ca mamaṃ bhava. ॥17॥ ^0-34

Tesaṃ saccena sīlena, khanti mettā balena ca. ^0-35

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena ca. ॥18॥ ^0-36

Purathimasmiṃ disābhāge, santi bhūtā mahiddhikā. ^0-37

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena ca. ॥19॥ ^0-38

Dakkhiṇasmiṃ disābhāge, santi devā mahiddhikā. ^0-39

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena ca. ॥20॥ ^0-40

Pacchimasmiṃ disābhāge, santi nāgā mahiddhikā. ^0-41

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena ca. ॥21॥ ^0-42

Uttarāsmiṃ disābhāge, santi yakkhā mahiddhikā. ^0-43

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena ca. ॥22॥ ^0-44

Purathimena Dhataraṭṭho, dakkhiṇena Virūḷhako. ^0-45

Pacchimena Virūpakkho, Kuvero uttaraṃ disaṃ. ॥23॥ ^0-46

Cattāro te mahārājā, lokapālā yasassino. ^0-47

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena ca. ॥24॥ ^0-48

Ākāsaṭṭhā ca bhummaṭṭhā, devā nāgā mahiddhikā. ^0-49

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena ca. ॥25॥ ^0-50

Iddhimanto ca ye devā, vasantā idha sāsane. ^0-51

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena ca. ॥26॥ ^0-52

Sabbītiyo vivajjantu, soko rogo vinassatu. ^0-53

Mā te bhavatvarāyo, sukhī dīghāyuko bhava. ॥27॥ ^0-54

Abhivādanasīlissa, niccaṃ vaddhāpacāyino. ^0-55

Cattāro dhammā vaḍḍhanti, āyu vaṇṇo sukhaṃ balaṃti. ॥28॥ ^0-56
