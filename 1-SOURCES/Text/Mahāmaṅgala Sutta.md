# Mahāmaṅgala Sutta ^0

Evaṃ me sutaṃ— ^0-1

Ekaṃ samayaṃ Bhagavā Sāvatthiyaṃ viharati Jetavane Anāthapiṇḍikassa ārāme. ^0-2

Atha kho aññatarā devatā abhikkantāya rattiyā abhikkantavaṇṇā kevalakappaṃ Jetavanaṃ obhāsetvā yena Bhagavā tenupasaṅkami. ^0-3

Upasaṅkamitvā Bhagavantaṃ abhivādetvā ekamantaṃ aṭṭhāsi. ^0-4

Ekamantaṃ ṭhitā kho sā devatā Bhagavantaṃ gāthāya ajjhabhāsi— ^0-5

Bahū devā manussā ca, ^0-6

Maṅgalāni acintayuṃ; ^0-7

Ākaṅkhamānā sotthānaṃ, ^0-8

Brūhi maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-9

Asevanā ca bālānaṃ, ^0-10

Paṇḍitānañca sevanā; ^0-11

Pūjā ca pūjaneyyānaṃ, ^0-12

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-13

Patirūpadesavāso ca, ^0-14

Pubbe ca katapuññatā; ^0-15

Attasammāpaṇidhi ca, ^0-16

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-17

Bāhusaccañca sippañca, ^0-18

Vinayo ca susikkhito; ^0-19

Subhāsitā ca yā vācā, ^0-20

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-21

Mātāpitu upaṭṭhānaṃ, ^0-22

Puttadārassa saṅgaho; ^0-23

Anākulā ca kammantā, ^0-24

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-25

Dānañca dhammacariyā ca, ^0-26

Ñātakānañca saṅgaho; ^0-27

Anavajjāni kammāni, ^0-28

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-29

Āratī viratī pāpā, ^0-30

Majjapānā ca saṃyamo; ^0-31

Appamādo ca dhammesu, ^0-32

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-33

Gāravo ca nivāto ca, ^0-34

Santuṭṭhi ca kataññutā; ^0-35

Kālena dhammassavanaṃ, ^0-36

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-37

Khantī ca sovacassatā, ^0-38

Samaṇānañca dassanaṃ; ^0-39

Kālena dhammasākacchā, ^0-40

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-41

Tapo ca brahmacariyañca, ^0-42

Ariyasaccāna dassanaṃ; ^0-43

Nibbānasacchikiriyā ca, ^0-44

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-45

Phuṭṭhassa lokadhammehi, ^0-46

Cittaṃ yassa na kampati; ^0-47

Asokaṃ virajaṃ khemaṃ, ^0-48

Etaṃ maṅgalamuttamaṃ. ^0-49

Etādisāni katvāna, ^0-50

Sabbatthamaparājitā; ^0-51

Sabbattha sotthiṃ gacchanti, ^0-52

Taṃ tesaṃ maṅgalamuttamanti. ^0-53

Maṅgalasuttaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ. ^0-54
