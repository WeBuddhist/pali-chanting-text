# Aṭṭhasīla Yācanā ^0

Okāsa, ahaṃ Bhante, tisaraṇena saha aṭṭhaṅgasamannāgataṃ uposathasīlaṃ dhammaṃ  yācāmi. ^0-1

Anuggahaṃ katvā sīlaṃ detha me Bhante. ^0-2

Okāsa, dutiyampi ahaṃ Bhante, tisaraṇena saha aṭṭhaṅgasamannāgataṃ uposathasīlaṃ dhammaṃ yācāmi. ^0-3

Anuggahaṃ katvā sīlaṃ detha me Bhante. ^0-4

Okāsa, tatiyampi ahaṃ Bhante, tisaraṇena saha aṭṭhaṅgasamannāgataṃ uposathasīlaṃ dhammaṃ  yācāmi. ^0-5

Anuggahaṃ katvā sīlaṃ detha me Bhante. ^0-6
