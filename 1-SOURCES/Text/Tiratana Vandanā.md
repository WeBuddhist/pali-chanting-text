# Tiratana Vandanā ^0

**Buddha Vandanā** ^0-1

Iti pi so Bhagavā Arahaṃ Sammāsambuddho. ^0-2

Vijjācaraṇasampanno Sugato Lokavidū. ^0-3

Anuttaro Purisadammasārathī. ^0-4

Satthā Devamanussānaṃ Buddho Bhagavāti. ^0-5

**Dhamma Vandanā** ^0-6

Svākkhāto Bhagavatā Dhammo. ^0-7

Sandiṭṭhiko Akāliko. ^0-8

Ehipassiko Opanayiko. ^0-9

Paccattaṃ Veditabbo Viññūhīti. ^0-10

**Saṅgha Vandanā** ^0-11

Supaṭipanno Bhagavato Sāvakasaṅgho. ^0-12

Ujupaṭipanno Bhagavato Sāvakasaṅgho. ^0-13

Ñāyapaṭipanno Bhagavato Sāvakasaṅgho. ^0-14

Sāmīcipaṭipanno Bhagavato Sāvakasaṅgho. ^0-15

Yadidaṃ Cattāri Purisayugāni, Aṭṭha Purisapuggalā. ^0-16

Esa Bhagavato Sāvakasaṅgho. ^0-17

Āhuneyyo Pāhuneyyo Dakkhiṇeyyo Añjalikaraṇīyo. ^0-18

Anuttaraṃ Puññakkhettaṃ Lokassāti. ^0-19

Sādhu! Sādhu! Sādhu! ^0-20
