# Jayamaṅgala Aṭṭhagāthā ^0

Bāhuṃ sahassamabhinimmita-sāvudhantaṃ ^0-1

Girimekhalaṃ uditaghora-sasenamāraṃ ^0-2

Dānādi-dhamma-vidhinā jitavā Munindo ^0-3

Taṃ tejasā bhavatu te jaya-maṅgalāni. ^0-4

Mārātirekamabhiyujjhita-sabba-rattiṃ ^0-5

Ghorampaṇāḷavakamakkhama-thaddha-yakkhaṃ ^0-6

Khantī-sudanta-vidhinā jitavā Munindo ^0-7

Taṃ tejasā bhavatu te jaya-maṅgalāni. ^0-8

Nāḷāgiriṃ gajavaraṃ atimatta-bhūtaṃ ^0-9

Dāvaggi-cakkamasanīva sudāruṇantaṃ ^0-10

Mettambuseka-vidhinā jitavā Munindo ^0-11

Taṃ tejasā bhavatu te jaya-maṅgalāni. ^0-12

Ukkhitta-khaggam-atihattha-sudāruṇantaṃ ^0-13

Dhāvantiyojanapathaṅgulimālavantaṃ ^0-14

Iddhībhisaṅkhata-mano jitavā Munindo ^0-15

Taṃ tejasā bhavatu te jaya-maṅgalāni. ^0-16

Katvāna kaṭṭhamudaraṃ iva gabbhiniyā ^0-17

Ciñcāya duṭṭhavacanaṃ janakāya-majjhe ^0-18

Santena soma-vidhinā jitavā Munindo ^0-19

Taṃ tejasā bhavatu te jaya-maṅgalāni. ^0-20

Saccaṃ vihāya mati-saccaka-vāda-ketuṃ ^0-21

Vādābhiropita-manaṃ ati-andha-bhūtaṃ ^0-22

Paññā-padīpa-jalito jitavā Munindo ^0-23

Taṃ tejasā bhavatu te jaya-maṅgalāni. ^0-24

Nandopananda-bhujagaṃ vibudhaṃ mahiddhiṃ ^0-25

Puttena thera-bhujagena damāpayanto ^0-26

Iddhūpadesa-vidhinā jitavā Munindo ^0-27

Taṃ tejasā bhavatu te jaya-maṅgalāni. ^0-28

Duggāha-diṭṭhi-bhujagena sudaṭṭha-hattaṃ ^0-29

Brahmaṃ visuddhi-jutimiddhi-Bakābhidhānaṃ ^0-30

Ñāṇāgadena vidhinā jitavā Munindo ^0-31

Taṃ tejasā bhavatu te jaya-maṅgalāni. ^0-32

Etāpi Buddha-jaya-maṅgala-aṭṭhagāthā ^0-33

Yo vācako dine-dine sarate matandī ^0-34

Hitvāna neka-vividhāni cupaddavāni ^0-35

Mokkhaṃ sukhaṃ adhigameyya naro sapañño. ^0-36
