# Nidhikaṇḍa Sutta ^0

Nidhiṃ nidheti puriso, gambhīre odakantike. ^0-1

Atthe kicce samuppanne, atthāya me bhavissati. ^0-2

Rājato vā duruttassa, corato pīḷitassa vā. ^0-3

Iṇassa vā pamokkhāya, dubbhikkhe āpadāsu vā. ^0-4

Etadatthāya lokasmiṃ, nidhi nāma nidhīyati. ^0-5

Tāvassunihito santo, gambhīre odakantike. ^0-6

Na sabbo sabbadā eva, tassa taṃ upakappati. ^0-7

Nidhi vā ṭhānā cavati, saññā vāssa vimuyhati. ^0-8

Nāgā vā apanāmenti, yakkhā vāpi haranti naṃ. ^0-9

Appiyā vāpi dāyādā, uddharanti apassato. ^0-10

Yadā puññakkhayo hoti, sabbametaṃ vinassati. ^0-11

Yassa dānena sīlena, saṃyamena damena ca. ^0-12

Nidhī sunihito hoti, itthiyā purisassa vā. ^0-13

Cetiyamhi ca saṅghe vā, puggale atithīsu vā. ^0-14

Mātari pitari cāpi, atho jeṭṭhamhi bhātari. ^0-15

Eso nidhi sunihito, ajeyyo anugāmiko. ^0-16

Pahāya gamanīyesu, etaṃ ādāya gacchati. ^0-17

Asādhāraṇamaññesaṃ, acorāharaṇo nidhi. ^0-18

Kayirātha dhīro puññāni, yo nidhi anugāmiko. ^0-19

Esa devamanussānaṃ, sabbakāmadado nidhi. ^0-20

Yaṃ yadevābhipatthenti, sabbametena labbhati. ^0-21

Suvaṇṇatā susaratā, susaṇṭhānā surūpatā. ^0-22

Ādhipaccaparivāro, sabbametena labbhati. ^0-23

Padesarajjaṃ issariyaṃ, cakkavattisukhaṃ piyaṃ. ^0-24

Devarajjampi dibbesu, sabbametena labbhati. ^0-25

Mānussikā ca sampatti, devaloke ca yā rati. ^0-26

Yā ca nibbānasampatti, sabbametena labbhati. ^0-27

Mittasampadamāgamma, yonisova payuñjato. ^0-28

Vijjāvimuttivasībhāvo, sabbametena labbhati. ^0-29

Paṭisambhidā vimokkhā ca, yā ca sāvakapāramī. ^0-30

Paccekabodhi buddhabhūmi, sabbametena labbhati. ^0-31

Evaṃ mahatthikā esā, yadidaṃ puññasampadā. ^0-32

Tasmā dhīrā pasaṃsanti, paṇḍitā katapuññatan'ti. ^0-33

Nidhikaṇḍasuttaṃ niṭṭhitaṃ. ^0-34
