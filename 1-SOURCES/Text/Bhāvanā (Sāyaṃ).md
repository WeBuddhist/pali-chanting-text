# Bhāvanā ^0

Devo vassatu kālena, sassa-sampatti hetu ca. ^0-1

Phīto bhavatu loko ca, rājā bhavatu dhammiko. ^0-2

Imāya Buddha-pūjāya katāya suddha-cetasā, ^0-3

Ciraṃ tiṭṭhatu Saddhammo, loko hotu sukhī sadā. ^0-4

Imāya Dhamma-pūjāya katāya suddha-cetasā, ^0-5

Ciraṃ tiṭṭhatu Saddhammo, loko hotu sukhī sadā. ^0-6

Imāya Saṅgha-pūjāya katāya suddha-cetasā, ^0-7

Ciraṃ tiṭṭhatu Saddhammo, loko hotu sukhī sadā. ^0-8
