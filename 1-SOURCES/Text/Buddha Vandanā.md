# Buddha Vandanā ^0

Arahaṃ Sammāsambuddho Bhagavā, Buddhaṃ Bhagavantaṃ abhivādemi. ^0-1

Svākkhāto Bhagavatā Dhammo, Dhammaṃ namassāmi. ^0-2

Supaṭipanno Bhagavato Sāvakasaṅgho, Saṅghaṃ namāmi. ^0-3

Namo Tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa. ^0-4

Namo Tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa. ^0-5

Namo Tassa Bhagavato Arahato Sammāsambuddhassa. ^0-6

**Puppha Pūjā** ^0-7

Vaṇṇagandhaguṇopetaṃ, etaṃ kusumasantatiṃ. ^0-8

Pūjayāmi Munindassa, Sirīpādasaroruhe. ^0-9

Pūjemi Buddhaṃ Kusumena Nena, ^0-10

Puññena Mettena Labhāmi Mokkhaṃ. ^0-11

Pupphaṃ Milāyāti Yathā Idaṃ Me, ^0-12

Kāyo Tathā Yāti Vināsabhāvaṃ. ^0-13

**Dhūpa Pūjā** ^0-14

Gandhasambhārayuttena, ^0-15

Dhūpenāhaṃ Sugandhinā. ^0-16

Pūjaye Pūjanīyantaṃ, ^0-17

Pūjābhājanamuttamaṃ. ^0-18

**Gandha Pūjā** ^0-19

Sugandhikāya Vadanaṃ, ^0-20

Anantaguṇagandhinā. ^0-21

Sugandhināhaṃ Gandhena, ^0-22

Pūjayāmi Tathāgataṃ. ^0-23

**Padīpa Pūjā** ^0-24

Ghanasārappadittena, ^0-25

Dīpena Tamadhaṃsinā. ^0-26

Tilokadīpaṃ Sambuddhaṃ, ^0-27

Pūjayāmi Tamonudaṃ. ^0-28

Sādhu! Sādhu! Sādhu! ^0-29
