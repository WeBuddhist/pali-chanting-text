# Bodhisattaguṇaṃ ^0

Sakala vijjaṃ vidhura jāṇaṃ, deva rūpaṃ sujīvaṃ. ^0-1

Vimalacakkhu gambhira ghoṃsaṃ, gaura vaṇṇaṃ sūkāyaṃ. ^0-2

Abhaya cittaṃ nibhaya kāmaṃ, sūrata dhammaṃ supemaṃ. ^0-3

Virata rajjaṃ sūjana netaṃ, bodhisattaṃ sarāmi. ^0-4

Bodhisattaṃ sarāmi, Bodhisattaṃ sarāmi. ^0-5
