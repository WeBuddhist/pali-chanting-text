# Sīvali Paritta ^0

Puretvā pāramī sabbe, sabbe pacceka nāyakā. ^0-1

Sīvali guṇa tejena, parittaṃ taṃ bhaṇāmahe. ^0-2

Natāsise ca mosīse, nānājālīti sajjalī. ^0-3

Sadeva manussa pūjitaṃ, sabba lābhā bhavantu te. ^0-4

Sīvali ca mahāthero, so raho paccayādinaṃ. ^0-5

Piyo devamanussānaṃ, piyo brāhmaṇamuttamaṃ. ^0-6

Piyo nāga supaṇṇānaṃ, pīnindriyaṃ namāmahaṃ. ^0-7

Padumuttaro nāma jino, sabba dhammesu cakkhumā. ^0-8

Ito sata sahassamhi, kappe uppajji nāyako. ^0-9

Sattāhaṃ dvāra mūḷho haṃ, mahādukkha samappito. ^0-10

Mātā me chanda dānena, evamāsi sudukkhitā. ^0-11

Kesesu chijjamānesu, arahattamapāpuṇiṃ. ^0-12

Devā nāgā manussā ca, paccayānup anenti te. ^0-13

Padumuttara nāmañca, Vipassissa vināyakaṃ. ^0-14

Sampūjayaṃ pamudito, paccayehi visesato. ^0-15

Tato tesaṃ visesena, kammānaṃ vipuluttamaṃ. ^0-16

Lābhaṃ labhāmi sabbattha, vane gāme jale thale. ^0-17

Tadā devā paṇītehi, mamatthāya mahāmati. ^0-18

Paccayehi mahāvīro, sasaṅgho loka nāyako. ^0-19

Upadiṭṭhā mayā buddho, gantvā Revatamassa. ^0-20

Tato Jetavanaṃ gantvā, etadaggaṭṭhapesi maṃ. ^0-21

Revataṃ dassanatthāya, yadāyāti vināyako. ^0-22

Tiṃsa bhikkhu sahassehi, saha lokaṃ nāyako. ^0-23

Lābhinaṃ Sīvali aggo, mama sissesu bhikkhavo. ^0-24

Sabbaloka hito satthā, kittayī parisāsu maṃ. ^0-25

Kilesā jhāpitā mayhaṃ, bhavā sabbe samūhatā. ^0-26

Nāgova bandhanaṃ chetvā, viharāmi anāsavo. ^0-27

Svāgataṃ vata me āsi, buddhaseṭṭhassa santike. ^0-28

Tisso vijjā anuppattā, kataṃ buddhassa sāsane. ^0-29

Paṭisambhidā catasso ca, vimokkhāpi ca aṭṭhame. ^0-30

Chaḷabhiññā sacchikatā, kataṃ buddhassa sāsane. ^0-31

Buddhaputto mahāthero, Sīvali jinasāvako. ^0-32

Uggatejo mahāvīro, tejasā jinasāsane. ^0-33

Rakkhanto sīla tejena, dhanavanto yasassino. ^0-34

Evaṃ tejānubhāvena, sadā rakkhatu Sīvali. ^0-35

Kappaṭāyitī buddhassa, bodhimūle nisīdiya. ^0-36

Mārasenappamaddanto, sadā rakkhatu Sīvali. ^0-37

Dasa pāramitāpatto, pabbaji jinasāsane. ^0-38

Gotama sakyaputtosi, therena mama Sīvali. ^0-39

Mahāsāvakā asītisu, Puṇṇathero yasassiso. ^0-40

Bhavabhoga aggalābhīsu, uttamo tena Sīvali. ^0-41

Evaṃ acintiyā Buddhā, Buddhadhammā acintiyā. ^0-42

Acintiyesu pasannānaṃ, vipāko hoti acintiyo. ^0-43

Tesaṃ saccena sīlena, khanti metta balena ca. ^0-44

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, sabba dukkha vināsanaṃ. ^0-45

Tesaṃ saccena sīlena, khanti metta balena ca. ^0-46

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, sabba bhaya vināsanaṃ. ^0-47

Tesaṃ saccena sīlena, khanti metta balena ca. ^0-48

Tepi maṃ anurakkhantu, sabba roga vināsanaṃ. ^0-49
