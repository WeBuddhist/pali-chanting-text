# Paṭiccasamuppāda ^0

Avijjāpaccayā saṅkhārā. ^0-1

Saṅkhārapaccayā viññāṇaṃ. ^0-2

Viññāṇapaccayā nāmarūpaṃ. ^0-3

Nāmarūpapaccayā saḷāyatanaṃ. ^0-4

Saḷāyatanapaccayā phasso. ^0-5

Phassapaccayā vedanā. ^0-6

Vedanāpaccayā taṇhā. ^0-7

Taṇhāpaccayā upādānaṃ. ^0-8

Upādānapaccayā bhavo. ^0-9

Bhavapaccayā jāti. ^0-10

Jātipaccayā jarāmaraṇaṃ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā sambhavanti. ^0-11

Evam etassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa samudayo hoti. ^0-12

Avijjāya tveva asesavirāganirodhā saṅkhāranirodho. ^0-13

Saṅkhāranirodhā viññāṇanirodho. ^0-14

Viññāṇanirodhā nāmarūpanirodho. ^0-15

Nāmarūpanirodhā saḷāyatananirodho. ^0-16

Saḷāyatananirodhā phassanirodho. ^0-17

Phassanirodhā vedanānirodho. ^0-18

Vedanānirodhā taṇhānirodho. ^0-19

Taṇhānirodhā upādānanirodho. ^0-20

Upādānanirodhā bhavanirodho. ^0-21

Bhavanirodhā jātinirodho. ^0-22

Jātinirodhā jarāmaraṇaṃ sokaparidevadukkhadomanassupāyāsā nirujjhanti. ^0-23

Evam etassa kevalassa dukkhakkhandhassa nirodho hoti. ^0-24
