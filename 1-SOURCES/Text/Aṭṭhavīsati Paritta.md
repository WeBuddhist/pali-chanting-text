# Aṭṭhavīsati Paritta ^0

Taṇhaṅkaro mahāvīro, Medhaṅkaro mahāyaso. ^0-1

Saraṇaṅkaro lokahito, Dīpaṅkaro jutindriyo. ^0-2

Koṇḍañño janapāmokkho, Maṅgalo parisāsabho. ^0-3

Sumano sumano dhīro, Revato rati vaṅgano. ^0-4

Sobhito guṇasampanno, Anomadassī januttamo. ^0-5

Padumo loka pajjoto, Nārado vara sārathī. ^0-6

Padumuttaro sattasāro, Sumedho aggapuggalo. ^0-7

Sujāto sabba lokaggo, Piyadassī narāsabho. ^0-8

Atthadassī kāruṇiko, Dhammadassī tamonudo. ^0-9

Siddhattho asamo loke, Tisso varada saṃvaro. ^0-10

Phusso varadasambuddho, Vipassī ca anupamo. ^0-11

Sikhī sabbahito satthā, Vessabhū sukhadāyako. ^0-12

Kakusandho satyavāho, Koṇāgamano raṇaṃjaho. ^0-13

Kassapo siri sampanno, Gotamo sakyapuṅgavo. ^0-14

Tesaṃ saccena sīlena, khanti metta balena ca. ^0-15

Tepi tvaṃ anurakkhantu, ārogyena sukhena cāti. ^0-16
